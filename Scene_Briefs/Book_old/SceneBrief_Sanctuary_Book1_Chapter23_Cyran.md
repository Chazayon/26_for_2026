#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Following the public defiance, Thessen retaliates by orchestrating a massive leak of Cyran's real, unsanitized temple history. Citizens and Vigil colleagues react with disgust. Cyran is painted as a manipulative, corruptive seducer, and Lysa as his naive victim. Confronted by this reality, Cyran's deeply ingrained trauma—that he ruins everyone he touches—resurfaces violently. Cyran begins pulling away emotionally, using his old defensive mechanisms of humor and distance.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran: Try to absorb the blow of his exposed past without letting Lysa see how completely it shatters him.

**Scene Outcome:**  
He succeeds in hiding his devastation, but the cost is driving the first wedge between him and Lysa.

**Scene Question:**  
Will Cyran revert to his old coping mechanisms when his darkest fears are realized?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
None immediate, but the damage to Lysa's reputation is compounding by the hour.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *I thought my past was buried, but in this city, even the dead have a way of digging themselves out.*
2. *The look on the vendor’s face is one I haven’t seen in a century: pure, unadulterated revulsion.*
3. *They didn't just leak the truth; they painted it in blood for the whole city to see.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Cyran walks his usual morning patrol route through the Ribcage District.
2. He notices the citizens staring, not with the usual fearful respect, but with absolute disgust.
3. A fellow Vigil protector spits on the ground as Cyran passes.
4. Cyran picks up a scattered broadsheet blowing across the plaza.
5. He reads the headline: "The Leech of the Temple: Blood Relic's Century of Depravity."
6. His heart drops as he scans the details—they are horribly accurate, not exaggerated.
7. He realizes the records came from the restricted Halo Ward Archives.
8. Panic sets in: he realizes the story paints Lysa as his latest "victim" to be corrupted.
9. He retreats from the public eye, slipping into the shadows of a bone-tunnel.
10. He meets Lysa near the Tribunal steps. She is already holding a copy of the broadsheet.
11. He immediately goes on the defensive, tossing a cruel, flippant joke about his own reputation.
12. Lysa doesn't laugh; she looks at him with profound concern, not disgust.
13. He cannot handle her pity. He deflects again, putting physical space between them.
14. She attempts to touch his arm; he subtly steps out of reach.
15. He tells her, "This is what happens when you play with temple ornaments, darling."
16. She tells him to stop using that word.
17. A junior investigator named Ilan passes by, muttering about "corroborating the leak."
18. Cyran's internal monologue spirals: he believes he is a poison that will ruin Lysa's life.
19. He convinces himself that his old "no way" belief was right all along.
20. He offers to resign, to disappear to protect her career.
21. She fiercely rejects the idea, insisting they fight it.
22. He smiles his practiced, hollow smile. "Whatever you say, Scribe."
23. The emotional distance is now a tangible wall between them.
24. He walks away to finish his patrol, alone.
25. He realizes, with sick certainty, that he must leave her to save her.

**Emotional Beats:**

1. **Confusion:** The public's sudden hostile reaction.
2. **Horror:** Reading the leaked details.
3. **Panic:** Realizing Lysa is implicated.
4. **Self-Loathing:** Believing the worst about himself.
5. **Defensive Retreat:** Pushing Lysa away to protect himself (and her).

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Cyran regresses, allowing his core lie ("I ruin everything") to dictate his actions rather than his newly developing trust.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
**Withdrawal:** The previously solid partnership shows its first crack as Cyran refuses to let Lysa comfort him.

**Subtext Layer:**
