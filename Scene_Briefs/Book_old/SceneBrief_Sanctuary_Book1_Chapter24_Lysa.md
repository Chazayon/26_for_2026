#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Lysa is ordered to meet with the junior investigator assigned to corroborate the allegations against Cyran. She meets Ilan (Series Cameo/Book 3 lead). Ilan processes crime scenes by touching them and "seeing" the magical residue. He reveals that someone is using old temple magic to fake the crimes Cyran is accused of, and that the signature belongs to the "Night of Shattered Vows." Dr. Marrow appears as Ilan's polite but deeply unsettling supervisor. Lysa realizes the conspiracy goes much deeper than just Thessen. 

**Scene Goal:**  
Lysa: Defend Cyran's innocence and figure out what the Tribunal actually knows.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Lysa gets proof of Cyran's innocence but realizes the villains are operating on a terrifyingly high magical level. She is left extremely unsettled by Dr. Marrow.

**Scene Question:**  
Will Ilan's investigation damn Cyran or offer a lifeline?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
The Tribunal preparing formal charges based on the leaked documents.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *The new investigator doesn't look at files; he looks at the empty spaces where the violence used to be.*
2. *They say Ilan sees too much, but right now, I’m hoping he sees exactly enough to save Cyran’s life.*
3. *Investigating magic in a dead god's city is like staring too long at the sun.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa arrives at a sealed-off alleyway where one of the disputed murders took place.
2. She finds a young man mostly sitting on the ground, touching a bloodstain with bare fingers.
3. She introduces herself; he doesn't look up, only nods and states her full name and title.
4. "Ilan," he says, finally standing. He wipes his hands, twitchy and unfocused.
5. She demands to know the status of the "corruption" allegations.
6. Ilan tilts his head. "The blood is wrong. The fear is wrong."
7. He touches the bone wall. "Someone is painting with old temple magic. It's not your Relic."
8. Lysa breathes a sigh of relief. "You'll put that in your report?"
9. Ilan frowns. "The signature... it's heavy. Tastes like ash and broken oaths."
10. He mutters, "I've seen this pattern before. My first case. The Night of Shattered Vows."
11. Lysa asks what that means; Ilan looks physically pained trying to explain.
12. A smooth, perfectly cultured voice interrupts: "I wouldn't press him, Scribe. He's had a long day."
13. Dr. Marrow steps into the alley. Immaculate suit, carrying an umbrella despite the dry weather.
14. Marrow introduces himself as Ilan's medical supervisor/handler from the Halo Ward.
15. Marrow smiles at Lysa—it's a smile that makes her skin crawl.
16. "We are simply here to ensure the truth is documented," Marrow says smoothly.
17. Lysa asks if the truth includes exonerating Cyran.
18. Marrow sighs delicately. "The truth is rarely that focused on one man, my dear."
19. Ilan is visibly subdued in Marrow's presence, retreating into himself.
20. Marrow offers Lysa a handkerchief (she didn't realize she was bleeding from a scraped knuckle).
21. She refuses it, putting her hands behind her back.
22. Marrow notes her reaction with quiet amusement. "Take care of your monster, Scribe. The old faiths are waking up."
23. Marrow places a hand on Ilan's shoulder and steers him away.
24. Lysa is left alone in the alley, clutching Ilan's preliminary notes.
25. She turns to head back, realizing she needs to warn Cyran that Varos is pulling strings far above Thessen's paygrade.

3. **Deep Unease:** Dr. Marrow's arrival.
4. **Protective Instinct:** Wanting to shield Ilan (and herself) from Marrow.
5. **Dread:** Marrow's parting words about the "old faiths."

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Lysa expands her view from a localized bureaucratic fight to a massive, magical conspiracy.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
**Lysa & Ilan:** Briefly maternal/protective, recognizing a gifted but damaged person.

**Subtext Layer:**
