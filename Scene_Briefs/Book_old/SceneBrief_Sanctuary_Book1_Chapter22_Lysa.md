#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
A public ceremony is held where Contract Lord Thessen attempts to invoke the old temple command language on Cyran, asserting ownership. Cyran, fueled by his new sense of self, struggles fiercely against the magic and refuses to obey. Lysa intervenes, stepping between them and invoking a newly discovered legal doctrine to block the command. The crowd watches as a scribe stands between a monster and a Lord. Cyran realizes no one owns him.

**Scene Goal:**  
Lysa: Protect Cyran from Thessen's command and establish legal precedent.

**Scene Outcome:**  
They publicly humiliate Thessen and establish their partnership as an unbreakable unit.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Lysa's legal maneuvers overcome Thessen's direct magical commands?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
Thessen's command magic taking root in Cyran's mind.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *Thessen’s voice carries the weight of chains, but I have the key.*
2. *A public ceremony is just an execution with better lighting.*
