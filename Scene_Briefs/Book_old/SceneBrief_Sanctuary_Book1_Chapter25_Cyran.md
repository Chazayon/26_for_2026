#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Convinced that the leaked documents and the looming conspiracy will completely destroy Lysa's career, reputation, and life, Cyran voices his deepest fear: "I'll ruin you." He explicitly suggests they end their relationship to protect her from the fallout. Lysa refuses to be pushed away, insisting they fight. But Cyran is too wrapped in his trauma to believe her. They reach an impasse, and for the first time since their relationship began, they sleep apart, the tension heavy and unresolved.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran: Push Lysa away to save her from his "toxicity."

**Scene Outcome:**  
He fails to make her leave, but succeeds in creating a massive, painful emotional rift.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Cyran overcome his self-hatred to trust Lysa's choice to stay with him?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
None immediate, this is a slow-burn emotional crisis.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *I have spent a century surviving ruins, and I refuse to let her become one.*
2. *The hardest part of a lie is making the person you love believe you don't care.*
3. *She looks at me like I am salvation, but I know I am the knife.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Cyran is in Lysa's quarters, pacing like a caged animal.
2. He is packing his few belongings into a small satchel.
3. Lysa arrives, holding the notes from her meeting with Ilan.
4. She stops in the doorway, seeing the bag. "What are you doing?"
5. He doesn't look at her. "Leaving. Before Thessen's leak completely burns your career down."
6. She drops her notes on the table. "I told you, we're fighting this. Ilan has proof—"
7. Cyran spins around, voice sharp. "Proof doesn't matter! The Tribunal doesn't care about proof, Lysa."
8. He walks to her, stopping right outside her personal space.
9. "They will strip your rank, ruin your name, and throw you to the wolves for associating with me."
10. She steps *into* his space. "That's my choice to make. Not yours."
11. He flinches. "You don't understand how deep the rot goes."
12. "Then explain it to me!" she demands.
13. He shakes his head, his face a mask of bitter certainty. "I ruin everyone who touches me. It is what I am."
14. She reaches for his hand; he pulls it away.
15. He voices the fear out loud: "I'll ruin you. And I can't live with that."
16. She tells him sharply, "Do not play the martyr. Do not do Varos's work for him by isolating yourself."
17. Her words hit too close to the truth. Cyran goes cold and quiet.
18. "It's a professional necessity," Cyran says, weaponizing his coldest, deadest voice.
19. Lysa looks at him, seeing through the act but deeply hurt by it.
20. "If you walk out that door," Lysa says quietly, "you're telling me you don't trust me to fight my own battles."
21. Cyran halts at the door, hand on the handle.
22. He cannot bring himself to leave her completely.
23. He tosses the bag onto a chair near the door.
24. "I'll sleep on the floor," he says, taking a blanket.
25. They turn out the lamps. Cyran lies in the darkness, staring at the ceiling, feeling an ocean of distance between them.

5. **Agonizing Isolation:** Lying on the floor, separate from her.
