#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Having barely escaped the "hearing" with his sanity intact, Cyran goes to Lysa's quarters. He has made his dreadful choice: he will surrender to Varos tomorrow to save her career and life. This is his final night with her. The resulting intimacy is desperate, intense, and underscored by his crushing, unspoken grief. She believes they are celebrating survival and affirming their bond after a terrible day; he knows he is saying goodbye. Afterward, he holds her as she sleeps, memorizing her before planning his exit.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran: Memorize exactly what it feels like to be loved as a man, not an object, before returning to his cage.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Intense emotional and physical intimacy that leaves Cyran completely shattered, cementing his resolve to leave.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Cyran go through with the goodbye without breaking down completely?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
Sunrise. When the sun comes up, he must leave.
