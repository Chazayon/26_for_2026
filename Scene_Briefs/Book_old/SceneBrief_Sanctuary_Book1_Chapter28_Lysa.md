#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Lysa wakes to find the bed empty and cold. On her desk is a brief, devastating note from Cyran: *"I won't be the thing that destroys you. Forgive me."* She realizes he hasn't just run away—he has gone to Varos, surrendering himself to protect her. She is furious and heartbroken, reading between the lines to see his sacrificial love. Spiraling, she goes to the Vein Markets to drink at Cairn's bar. Cairn observes her misery and offers gruff, crucial advice: *"When someone walks away to save you, the question is whether you let them."* This sparks her transition from grief to fury.

**Scene Goal:**  
Lysa: Process the betrayal of his sacrifice and decide what to do next.

**Scene Outcome:**  
She moves through the shock of abandonment and decides to fight for him, even if he doesn't want her to.

**Scene Question:**  
Will Lysa accept Cyran's sacrifice or choose to fight?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
None immediate; allowing the emotional devastation to land.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *The cold is the first thing that tells me he’s gone. The silence confirms it.*
2. *There are three sentences on the paper, and they dismantle my entire life.*
3. *I always knew he was a flight risk; I didn’t realize he would fly straight back to his cage.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa wakes up. The room feels hollow.
2. She reaches for Cyran's side of the bed; the sheets are cold.
3. She sits up, calling his name. No answer.
4. She spots his Vigil badge sitting on her desk, deliberately left behind.
5. Beside it is a folded piece of parchment.
6. She opens the note with trembling hands.
7. She reads the words: "I won't be the thing that destroys you. Forgive me."
8. She drops the paper. The realization hits: he isn't fleeing the city, he has surrendered to Varos.
9. Pure, blinding rage temporarily overrides her grief. "You absolute coward," she whispers.
10. She dresses mechanically, putting her uniform on like armor.
11. She leaves her quarters, walking aimlessly before realizing where her feet are taking her.
12. She arrives at the Vein Markets. The daylight makes the district look cheap and exhausted.
13. She pushes into Cairn's bar. It is mostly empty, smelling of stale ale and sawdust.
14. Cairn is wiping down the bar. He takes one look at her face and pours a glass of clear, harsh spirit.
15. He doesn't ask. He just slides the glass over.
16. She downs it. It burns like fire. She asks for another.
17. Cairn pours it. "He looked like a dead man walking when I saw him earlier," Cairn notes casually.
18. Lysa's head snaps up. "Where?"
19. "Heading towards the Spine. Heavy boots. No sword."
20. Lysa slumps over the bar, tears finally spilling. "He did it to protect me. Because he thinks I'm weak."
21. Cairn stops wiping the bar. He leans on the heavy wood, looking at her with grim experience.
22. "Don't flatter yourself," Cairn grunts. "He did it because he thinks *he's* poison."
23. Lysa wipes her face, stung by the truth of the assessment.
24. Cairn adds quietly, "When someone walks away to save you, Scribe, the only question that matters is whether you're going to let them."
25. Lysa stares at the bottom of her glass. Her grief hardens into iron resolve. She stands up.
