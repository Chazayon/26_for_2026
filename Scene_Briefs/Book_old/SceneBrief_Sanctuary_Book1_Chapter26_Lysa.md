#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Evidence emerges that could clear Cyran of the corruption allegations. Thessen uses it as bait to summon Cyran for a "hearing." It is actually an ambush involving old temple priests and contract magic. Lysa is physically barred from entering the chamber by Tribunal guards. Inside, Cyran faces Varos for the first time since his "death." Varos offers Cyran an agonizing choice: return to the temple willingly, or Varos will systematically destroy Lysa's career, reputation, and life.

**Scene Goal:**  
Lysa: Break into the hearing room and protect Cyran.

**Scene Outcome:**  
She fails to breach the doors, leaving Cyran isolated with his abuser and setting up his devastating choice.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Lysa bridge the physical and legal barriers the system puts between her and Cyran?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
The terrifying amount of time Cyran is left alone with Varos behind closed doors.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *The doors to the primary hearing chamber are solid iron, but the silence behind them is heavier.*
2. *They called it a hearing, which is Tribunal shorthand for an execution without witnesses.*
3. *I have spent my life defending the Tribunal's rules, and right now, I want to burn them all down.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa and Cyran arrive at the Tribunal halls to present Ilan's exculpatory evidence.
2. They are met by a phalanx of heavily armed guards, not clerks.
3. The captain of the guard orders Cyran inside alone; Lysa is barred from entry.
4. Lysa protests, citing tribunal law allowing legal representation.
5. The captain cites a higher, obscure "Temple exception" drafted by Thessen.
6. Cyran touches Lysa's arm. "Stay here. It's fine." (He's lying).
7. Cyran steps through the heavy iron doors; they slam shut with finality.
8. Lysa paces the corridor frantic, trying every legal argument she knows on the guards.
9. They remain impassive.
10. Inside the chamber (heard muffled through the doors), chanting starts. Old temple magic.
11. Lysa throws herself physically against the iron doors. They don't budge.
12. She yells Cyran's name, demanding they open the doors.
13. [Shift to Cyran's muffled perspective/Lysa's realization of what's happening]:
14. Varos's voice, not Thessen's, bleeds through the heavy wood.
15. Lysa goes cold with horror. Varos is alive, and he is here.
16. She presses her ear to the door.
17. Varos's muffled voice lays out the ultimatum: return to the temple, or Lysa is destroyed.
18. Lysa hammers on the door. "Don't listen to him! Cyran!"
19. Utter silence from Cyran inside. (He is absorbing the blow, accepting his fate).
20. The chanting reaches a crescendo, then suddenly stops.
21. The doors are wrenched open from the inside.
22. Cyran stands there, pale as a corpse, eyes blank and deadened.
23. Thessen smirks from behind him; Varos is gone, slipped out a side door.
24. Cyran walks past Lysa without looking at her, his posture rigid.
25. Lysa grabs his sleeve; he flinches away like she burned him, setting up his retreat.
