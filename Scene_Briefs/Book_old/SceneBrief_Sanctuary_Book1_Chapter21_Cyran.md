#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Cyran and Lysa have a plan to expose Varos and secure his freedom using legal precedent. Everything seems possible: love, freedom, justice. They attend a bone-market festival together, which feels like a real date. Cyran allows himself to imagine a future. They spot Cairn's bar looking busy and encounter Seraph, whose contract-collar reminds them of the cages still out there.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran: Enjoy the festival and the feeling of hope.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Cyran experiences genuine happiness, but the sight of Seraph's collar reminds him of the system they are fighting.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Cyran let his guard down and believe in a happy future?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
None immediate, this is a pause before the storm.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *I am rewriting the ending of my own story, and for the first time, I think I might like it.*
2. *The bone-market festival smells of roasting marrow, but tonight, it smells like freedom.*
3. *Hope is a dangerous muscle to exercise after centuries of atrophy.*

1. Lysa finishes drafting the legal documents that could set precedent for monster rights.

14. Lysa watches, not jealous, but observant.

17. Lysa describes it as a "funhouse mirror" of Cyran's past trauma.

19. He tells Seraph delicately, "There's a way out. It just takes longer than it should."
20. Seraph smiles, but it doesn't reach his eyes. He dances away.

22. The reminder of the system tempers his soaring hope.
23. They find a quiet spot away from the main thoroughfare.
24. Cyran kisses her, deeply and gratefully.
