#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Lysa meets **Ilan**, assigned to investigate the "corruption" allegations. He's clearly gifted and clearly unstable—touches evidence and sees things. His take: *"Someone's using old temple magic. It's not Cyran. The signature is wrong."* He also mentions: *"I've seen this pattern before. My first case. The Night of Shattered Vows."* Dr. Marrow appears as Ilan's "supervisor"—polite, helpful, subtly wrong. Lysa is unsettled but can't pinpoint why.

**Scene Goal:**  
Lysa must convince the eccentric Tribunal investigator (Ilan) that she isn't magically compromised by Cyran, while simultaneously trying to extract his forensic findings about the true killer (Varos) to clear Cyran's name.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Ilan confirms that Cyran is innocent of the murders, but introduces a far darker, older conspiracy tied to the "Night of Shattered Vows." Dr. Marrow’s unsettling intervention leaves Lysa doubting who she can trust within the Tribunal.

**Scene Question:**  
If the Tribunal knows Cyran is innocent of the murders, why are they still trying to destroy him and Lysa?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
Thessen's smear campaign is actively dismantling Lysa's career hour by hour. If she doesn't find hard evidence to counter the broadsheets soon, she will be officially stripped of her rank and arrested for corruption.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *I have spent my life reading ledgers, but Ilan reads the blood on the cobblestones like it’s a confession.*
2. *The problem with a brilliant investigator is that they usually see the knife before they notice who is holding the handle.*
3. *Dr. Marrow’s smile never quite reaches his eyes; it stops somewhere around his cheekbones, cold and perfectly calculated.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa paces the perimeter of a new crime scene in the lower Ribcage. She is officially suspended pending investigation, but she pushed her way past the Vigil guards anyway.
2. She spots **Ilan**, the junior investigator from yesterday, crouching in the center of the marrow-alley.
3. Ilan is not taking notes. He has his bare hands pressed flat against a bloodstained bone-pillar. He is violently shivering, his eyes squeezed shut.
4. Lysa steps closer. "Investigator Ilan."
5. Ilan gasps, yanking his hands away from the pillar as if burned. He scrubs his hands furiously on his trousers, breathing hard.
6. He looks up at her, eyes twitching. "Auditor Voss. You shouldn't be here. You're a bio-hazard right now. Politically speaking."
7. Lysa ignores the slight. She gestures to the pillar. "What did you see?"
8. Ilan pulls a pair of thin leather gloves from his pocket and snaps them on. "Fear. Mostly fear. And a harvest binding."
9. Lysa narrows her eyes. "Cyran didn't do this."
10. Ilan snorts, pulling out a charred piece of bone from an evidence bag. "Obviously. The Blood Relic's magic tastes like ozone and ash. This?" He taps the bag. "This tastes like myrrh and old rot. It's temple magic."
11. Relief floods Lysa. She has an ally who sees the truth. "Will you put that in your report?"
12. "I already did," Ilan says, his voice dropping. "But Voss won't read it. They don't care about the truth, Scribe. They care about the narrative."
13. Ilan steps closer, lowering his voice further. "I've seen this pattern before. The specific cut, the blood drain. My first case."
14. Lysa frowns. "When?"
15. "The Night of Shattered Vows," Ilan whispers, referencing the historic massacre that supposedly wiped out the old temple.
16. Before Lysa can ask him to elaborate, a smooth, cultured voice echoes down the alley. "Ilan, my boy. You are vibrating again. Pace yourself."
17. **Dr. Marrow** steps into the alley, immaculate in a white physician's coat, holding a silver-tipped cane.
18. Ilan immediately stiffens, stepping back from Lysa, his entire posture snapping into rigid, nervous deference.
19. Marrow smiles at Lysa—that cold, unblinking smile. "Auditor Voss. How unfortunate to see you caught in such a vulgar public scandal."
20. Lysa glares at him. "You authorized the release of Cyran's restricted files to Thessen."
21. Marrow sighs, examining the silver handle of his cane. "I merely provided historical context to a Lord of the city. Transparency is the bedrock of science, is it not?"
22. Lysa steps into Marrow's space. "It wasn't science. It was a targeted hit. You handed Thessen a weapon."
23. Marrow’s smile vanishes. For a fraction of a second, Lysa feels the overwhelming, suffocating presence of an apex predator.
24. "Be careful, Scribe," Marrow says softly. "Some weapons are too large for a Contract Lord to wield. Sometimes, the weapon breaks the man."
25. Marrow turns smoothly. "Come, Ilan. We have autopsies to complete." Ilan throws Lysa one last, desperate, warning look before following Marrow out of the alley. Lysa is left standing alone in the cold.

#### **Emotional Architecture**

**Emotional Beats (5-8 Key Moments):**

1. **Defiant Frustration:** Lysa trespassing on a crime scene she is technically banned from. Refusing to surrender.
2. **Morbid Fascination:** Watching Ilan use psychometry. Recognizing his raw, unstable power.
3. **Profound Relief:** Ilan validating Cyran's innocence and confirming Varos's magic.
4. **Dawning Horror:** Ilan connecting the current murders to the Night of Shattered Vows (a massive historical massacre).
5. **Instant Unease:** The arrival of Dr. Marrow. The visceral "wrongness" of his presence.
6. **Outrage:** Confronting Marrow about the leaked files that are destroying her life.
7. **Primal Fear:** Marrow dropping the polite mask for a microsecond. The realization that he is infinitely more dangerous than Thessen.
8. **Isolation:** Being left alone in the alley, realizing the conspiracy is deeper and darker than she ever imagined.

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Lysa realizes that her mastery of Tribunal law is useless against the ancient, monstrous forces (Varos, Marrow, the Prime Contract) currently moving on the board. She has to stop playing the auditor and start playing the revolutionary.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
**The Absent Anchor:** Cyran is not in this scene (he is retreating, as established in Ch 23). Lysa feels his absence acutely. She is fighting for him, but she is fighting alone. 

**Subtext Layer:**  
Marrow's line ("Sometimes, the weapon breaks the man") implies he *wants* Thessen to fail. He leaked the files not to help Thessen, but to force a catastrophic confrontation that Marrow intends to capitalize on.
