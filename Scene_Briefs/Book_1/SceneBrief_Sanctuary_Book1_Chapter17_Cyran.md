#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Neutral space: her quarters, no temple trappings, no opulence. Conversation about futures: What he'd do if redemption actually freed him (he doesn't know). What she'd do if the Tribunal didn't own her time (quiet admission of loneliness). He initiates physical closeness *without* debt: Asks to kiss her—asks, doesn't take. She says yes. Explicit negotiation of desires and boundaries. The scene: tender, slow, focused on consent and sensation. For him: the first time intimacy is about want, not survival. For her: the first time she lets herself be vulnerable.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran wants to bridge the physical gap between them, but he must completely unlearn his centuries of performative, transactional seduction to actually *be present* with her. 

**Scene Outcome:**  
They consummate their relationship in a profoundly healing, consent-driven encounter. Cyran realizes that pleasure does not have to be a performance, and his body belongs to him again.

**Scene Question:**  
Can Cyran engage in the very act weaponized against him for centuries without dissociating or falling into his "ornament" persona?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
The quiet of the night. Come morning, the Tribunal will hunt them, but in this room, time is suspended. 

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *I know exactly how to seduce a mark, but standing in the middle of her cramped, ink-stained room, I have absolutely no idea how to touch a woman I love.*
