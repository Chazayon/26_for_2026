#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Investigation day in the living district—Lysa researching temple history, Cyran as her guide. Citizens react to Cyran with fear, disgust, occasional gratitude. Light banter; a near-soft scene sharing street food in a bone-market stall. Cyran's internal "no way": *She'll see eventually. Everyone does. And then she'll be done.* He pushes her away verbally, testing—she doesn't budge. She asks about his past; he deflects with dark humor.

**Scene Goal:**  
Cyran wants to guide Lysa safely through the mortal markets to find temple history clues, while simultaneously trying to prove to her (and himself) that he belongs in the shadows, not beside her.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Cyran fails to drive Lysa away. Her refusal to treat him like a monster or a fragile victim deepens their bond, though he remains terrified of it.

**Scene Question:**  
How hard does Cyran have to push before Lysa gives up on him and sees the monster everyone else sees?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
Lysa’s temporary access pass to the outer archive outposts expires at sundown. They need to trace the old temple boundaries before the light fails.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *The problem with the living is that they are so dreadfully obvious about their heartbeats.*
2. *I am accustomed to crowds parting for me, but usually it involves more screaming and less awkward shuffling.*
3. *She walks through the mortal market like she belongs here, while I trail behind her like the shadow that will eventually swallow her whole.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Cyran trails Lysa through the bustling Hanging Gardens market, stepping carefully over trailing vines.
2. A mortal child runs across their path; Cyran sidesteps with preternatural speed.
3. The child's mother gasps, snatches the boy up, and backs against a wall, shielding her eyes.
4. Cyran performs a sweeping, mocking bow to the terrified mother. 
5. Lysa catches his sleeve, pulling him forward without a word.
6. They stop before a crumbling bone-archway covered in faded, ivy-choked temple script.
7. Lysa pulls out her notebook and begins tracing the eroded sigils with her fingers.
8. Cyran scans the rooftops, restless, cataloging three different pickpockets and a Vigil sniper in the distance.
9. A group of merchants sees Cyran and abruptly abandons their stall to hide in a marrow-alley.
10. Cyran leans against the archway, aggressively casual, watching Lysa work.
11. Lysa finishes her sketch, rubs her stomach, and points to a nearby, still-manned street food cart.
12. They approach the steaming cart; the vendor (Kael) drops his iron tongs with a clatter at the sight of Cyran.
13. Lysa orders two spiced gristle-skewers, completely ignoring the vendor's visible terror.
14. Cyran drops a silver Tribunal coin onto the wooden counter; Kael flinches as if it burned him.
15. Kael hands the skewers over with shaking hands, keeping his eyes glued to the floor.
16. Cyran and Lysa move to a relatively quiet alcove shaded by a massive, hollowed-out rib.
17. Lysa bites into her skewer, wiping a smudge of red cumin-spice from the corner of her mouth.
18. Cyran takes a slow bite of his own, masking his distaste for mortal food to mirror her actions.
19. He casually remarks that the meat tastes a bit too stringy, comparing it graphically to a god-spawn's thigh. 
20. Lysa doesn't gag or flinch; she simply chews, swallows, and critiques his attempt to gross her out.
21. Cyran's smirk falters; he tosses his half-eaten skewer into a nearby refuse brazier.
22. Lysa steps closer, stepping into his personal space, and asks a direct, blunt question about his time in the temple under Varos.
23. Cyran physically recoils, his spine hitting the bone wall behind him.
24. He deflects with a dark, bitter joke about his value as an ornamental centerpiece.
25. Lysa simply holds his gaze, taps her notebook against his chest, and turns to lead them to the next ruin.

#### **Emotional Architecture**

**Emotional Beats (5-8 Key Moments):**

1. **Hyper-Vigilance/Alienation:** The bright, loud mortal market makes him feel exposed and acutely aware of his monstrous nature.
2. **Defensive Theatre:** When the mother fears him, he leans into the "villain" persona to protect his pride.
3. **Fondness:** Watching Lysa work with complete focus, oblivious to the danger of standing next to him.
4. **Provocation (The Test):** Deliberately trying to disgust her with the meat comment to force her to reject him.
5. **Shock:** Her complete lack of revulsion. The test fails.
6. **Vulnerability:** Her direct question about the temple pierces his armor instantly.
7. **Panic/Retreat:** Using dark humor to build the wall back up before she sees too much.
8. **Lingering Awe:** Realizing she sees through the jokes but isn't leaving.

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Cyran actively tries to sabotage the connection because he believes his core lie ("I am only valuable for pleasure/violence, and eventually I ruin everything"). Her refusal to be sabotaged forces a tiny crack in that belief.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
**Testing Boundaries:** Cyran pushes. Lysa anchors. She establishes that his defense mechanisms won't work on her anymore.

**Subtext Layer:**  
Every time Cyran says, "I'm a monster," he is secretly begging her to say, "Not to me." When he makes the gross joke about the meat, he wants her to run away *now* so it hurts less than if she runs away *later*.

#### **World Interaction & Secondary Characters**

**Secondary Character Interactions:**

- **Kael the Street Vendor:** A middle-aged mortal man with a burn scar over one eye and a greasy apron. He runs a gristle-skewer cart in the Hanging Gardens.
- **Interaction:** Cyran approaches to buy food. Kael is terrified, dropping his tools. Cyran is perfectly, terrifyingly polite.
- **Voice/Mannerism:** Kael stammers, doesn't make eye contact, and his hands visibly shake when he takes the Tribunal coin. He mumbles, "Y-yes, m'lord Relic. Right away."

**Dialogue Moments:**

- **Worldbuilding:** Kael's use of "m'lord Relic" shows that the citizenry views the Vigil monsters as a twisted aristocracy—feared, but socially above poor mortals. 
- **Local Gossip:** While sketching earlier, they overhear merchants whispering that "Thessen's hounds are sniffing around the old shrines again."

**Character Interaction Beats:**

- **How Cyran treats them:** Cyran uses his smoothest, most aristocratic voice to order the food, deliberately playing into the "vampire lord" stereotype to mask his discomfort. He overpays, dropping silver instead of copper, but Kael is too scared to be grateful.
- **What it reveals:** Cyran is incredibly isolated. Even when he is just buying lunch, he is treated like a loaded gun. 

**Environmental Engagement:**

- **Background activity:** The market is loud—haggling, the chop of butcher knives on bone blocks, the rustle of the hanging vines swaying in the smoggy breeze.
- **Objects touched:** The hot, greasy wooden stick of the meat skewer; the eroded, chalky bone of the temple archway. 
- **Physical interaction:** Cyran traces the edge of a carved sigil while Lysa sketches it, his finger remembering the shape of the magic before his brain does.

**Location:** The Hanging Gardens Market (Upper tier of the Ribcage District).

**Time & Weather:**
- Time of day: 1:00 PM.
- Season: Autumn-chill.
- Weather conditions: The "sun" (or whatever ambient light the dead god provides) is at its zenith, casting harsh, highly-contrasted shadows through the rib-arches. 

**Sensory Environment (Required):**

- **Sights:** Bright, garish awnings strung between ribs; the glint of Lysa's spectacles; the greasy sheen on the roasted meat; the wide, white eyes of the frightened mother.
- **Sounds:** The sizzle of fat dropping onto Kael's brazier; the low murmur of the crowd that falls silent wherever Cyran steps; the scratch of Lysa's charcoal pencil.
- **Smells:** Heavy cumin and roasted fungus; the metallic tang of mortal fear-sweat; the sharp ozone smell lingering on the old temple ruins.
- **Textures:** The chalky dust of the bone archway rubbing off on Cyran's gloves; the sticky warmth of the skewer.
- **Taste:** The heavily spiced, slightly charred meat that tastes like ash to Cyran's vampiric palate, but which he chews anyway.
- **Temperature:** Uncomfortably warm in the sun, making Cyran feel sluggish and exposed.

**Spatial Blocking:**
- Cyran constantly positions himself between Lysa and the crowd, an unconscious bodyguard routine.
- During the "test" conversation, they are in a shaded alcove. He starts beside her, but physically retreats until his back hits the wall when she asks about his past.

**Props/Objects of Significance:**
- **The Skewer:** A symbol of mortal, mundane life that Cyran tries (and fails) to participate in.
- **The Silver Coin:** Proof of his "blood money" from the Tribunal.
- **Lysa's Notebook:** Her anchor to reality and truth.

**Obstacle/Complication:**

**Stakes:**
- **Personal:** If he pushes too hard, he loses the only person who has ever looked at him as a person. If he doesn't push hard enough, he risks opening up to someone who might eventually betray him.
