#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Lysa ascends from the cistern into the Ribcage district, navigating the foggy streets toward Cyran's quarters. Adrenaline crashes, leaving her to process the intense physical and emotional impact of his proximity. She analyzes his lethal, impossible beauty while confronting her own plainness, realizing with a shock of regret that for a split second, she had desperately wanted to accept his indecent proposal.

**Scene Goal:**  
To regain her professional composure, stop her hands from shaking, and reach Cyran's quarters before she loses her nerve.

**Scene Outcome:**  
She physically reaches his building, but her emotional armor is severely compromised by her own awakened desire and deep-seated insecurities.

**Scene Question:**  
Will Lysa's professionalism hold up against the undeniable, shocking physical attraction she feels for a monster she is supposed to be judging?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
She instructed him to meet her at his quarters. She has perhaps ten minutes to cross the district and armor herself before facing him again in a private setting.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**  
1. The phantom pressure of his blade is still resting against my throat, a cold line of steel that my pulse refuses to forget.
2. I don't start shaking until the heavy iron hatch of the cistern clangs shut behind me, sealing the god-blood away.
3. It takes exactly forty-two stairs for my professional indignation to burn off, leaving nothing behind but the terrifying, thudding heat of my own body.

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa pushes through the heavy iron access door, stepping out into the cold, marrow-mist of the Ribcage street.
2. She leans against the damp bone-wall, squeezing her eyes shut as a violent shudder of delayed adrenaline takes her.
3. She presses her ink-stained fingers to her throat, tracing the exact line where Cyran's dagger lay.
4. She forces herself to walk, boots clicking sharply on the uneven vertebrae-cobbles to drown out her racing heartbeat.
5. She passes a street-side puddle reflecting the sickly green moss-light; she stops to stare at her own reflection.
6. She attempts to smooth her frizzing, practical brown hair back into its severe braid, noting her pale, exhausted face.
7. She wipes a smear of tunnel grime from her cheek, her hands trembling slightly.
8. A passing night-cart clatters by; she flattens herself against a rib-arch to avoid the spray of dirty water.
9. A night-vendor selling roasted marrow-chestnuts calls out to her, offering a warm paper cone.
10. She pauses, buys the chestnuts, needing the grounding warmth against her cold palms.
11. The vendor spots her Tribunal badge under her cloak and immediately drops his gaze, his friendly demeanor vanishing into subservient fear.
12. Lysa thanks him quietly, the interaction leaving a sour taste in her mouth about her own institutional power.
13. She walks while peeling a chestnut, the heat burning her fingertips, grounding her in physical reality.
14. She eats the chestnut, the roasted, smoky flavor mixing with the lingering phantom scent of Cyran's expensive cologne.
15. She forcefully reviews the facts of the audit, pulling out her notebook under a streetlamp to read her own shaky handwriting.
16. The visual memory of Cyran hijacks her focus: the firelight catching in his bone-white hair, the predatory grace of his lean build.
17. She touches her own sensible, drab indigo robes, hyper-aware of how thoroughly she lacks his lethal glamour.
18. She halts in the shadow of a hanging bone-garden, gripped by a sudden, intense wave of physical arousal at the memory of his chest pressed to her back.
19. She drops the remaining chestnuts into a gutter, suddenly nauseous with shame.
20. A pair of Vigil wardens approach on patrol; Lysa flashes her badge, holding her spine rigid as they pass with respectful nods.
21. She forces her breathing to slow, counting her exhales, rebuilding the "Investigator Voss" persona brick by brick.
22. She reaches the base of Cyran's residential tower, looking up at the velvet-draped windows glowing with candlelight.
23. She pauses at the threshold, her hand hovering over the heavy bone-handle of the outer door.
24. She admits the terrifying truth to herself: for one second in the dark, she had wanted to say yes to his offer.
25. She grips the handle, turns it, and steps into the stairwell, choosing duty over desire.

#### **Emotional Architecture**

**Emotional Beats (5-8 Key Moments):**  
1. **Shock & Adrenaline:** The physical crash after surviving a knife at her throat.
2. **Hyper-awareness:** The sudden, overwhelming realization of Cyran's physical perfection and the scent of him clinging to her clothes.
3. **Insecurity/Self-Deprecation:** Looking at her drab reflection and feeling utterly inadequate and plain compared to him.
4. **Shameful Arousal:** The visceral body-memory of his chest against her back, making her flush in the cold air.
5. **Epiphany of Regret:** The horrifying realization that his offer of his body felt like a bribe, but she actually *wanted* it.
6. **Isolation:** The vendor's fear of her badge reminds her of the lonely, sterile life she leads.
7. **Fortification:** Locking the desire away in a mental box to survive the upcoming interview.
8. **Resolute dread:** Stepping into his building, knowing she is walking into a trap of her own making.

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Lysa realizes her impenetrable professional armor has a massive, gaping flaw. She is not immune to temptation, making her rigidly held morals a choice she has to actively fight for, rather than a passive state of being.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
Lysa realizes the power dynamic is vastly unbalanced. Institutionally, she holds his life in her hands. Physically and sexually, he completely eclipses her.

**Subtext Layer:**  
Her anger at Cyran isn't just because he tried to bribe her; it's because a being that beautiful could never *genuinely* desire someone as plain as her, making his offer inherently transactional and manipulative.
