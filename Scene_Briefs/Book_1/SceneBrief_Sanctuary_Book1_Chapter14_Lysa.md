#### **Plot & Structure**

**Plot Summary:**  
Lysa gains access to the Marrow Archives (in the Halo Ward, near Dr. Marrow's domain) using the key Meris gave her. She reads euphemistic records of Cyran's temple history—centuries of abuse disguised as holy duty. Cyran catches her researching; he tracked her, feeling exposed and betrayed that she is reading his "owner's manual." Confrontation ensues: *"Hungry for scandal, scribe? Want to know how many times they broke me?"* Lysa doesn't back down or apologize for seeking the truth. She offers an ultimatum: *"If you want to correct the story, you'll have to tell it yourself."* 

**Scene Goal:**  
Find a legal precedent in the old temple charters to block Thessen's Gala, while simultaneously trying to understand the root of Cyran's trauma.

**Scene Outcome:**  
Lysa uncovers the horrifying extent of Cyran's institutional abuse, but her investigation breaches his trust, forcing a confrontation that demands he take agency over his own narrative.

**Scene Question:**  
Will Lysa's pursuit of the truth drive Cyran away, or will it force him to finally drop the mask and share his real past?

**Ticking Clock/Deadline:**  
Approximately 48 hours remain on the legal delay she initiated at the Tribunal. Every hour spent reading is an hour lost to stop Thessen.

**Opening Hook - First Line Strategy:**

1. *The Marrow Archives smell exactly like the Tribunal, if the Tribunal had died a century ago and no one had bothered to bury it.*
2. *There is a special kind of evil in bureaucracy: the ability to turn atrocities into neat, alphabetized columns.*
3. *I turn the brass key in the lock, knowing that whatever I find on the other side is going to break my heart.*

#### **Action Beats (25 Minimum)**

1. Lysa navigates the clinical, brightly lit corridors of the Halo Ward, passing healers in white robes.
2. She locates the heavy iron door of the Marrow Archives and inserts the brass key given by Meris.
3. The door opens with a heavy groan, revealing endless rows of bone-shelving stretching into the dark.
4. She lights a lumen-sphere, illuminating dust motes and rows of preservation jars.
5. Footsteps echo behind her; she turns, hand on her stun-baton.
6. A tall, impeccably dressed man in a physician's coat steps from the shadows—Dr. Marrow.
7. Dr. Marrow smiles, setting down a silver tray with a steaming teapot and two porcelain cups on a reading desk.
8. He pours the tea, pushing a cup toward her, remarking on her "fascinating" choice of reading material.
9. Lysa accepts the cup but does not drink, watching his unnervingly unblinking eyes.
10. Marrow tidies a stack of nearby files, mentioning a junior investigator named Ilan who also "spends too much time looking at old wounds."
11. He bows politely and leaves her to her work, the door clicking shut behind him.
12. Lysa abandons the tea and moves to Section 4: *Temple Assets & Relic Acquisitions.*
13. She pulls down a massive, red-leather ledger, blowing a thick layer of dust from the cover.
14. She flips through the brittle parchment, her finger tracing down the index to Cyran's original designation number.
15. She begins to read the entries, translating the bureaucratic euphemisms ("consecration of the vessel," "compliance adjustments," "donor appeasement") into the horrific reality.
16. Her stomach turns; she covers her mouth, physically nauseated by a particularly detailed entry regarding his resurrection protocols.
17. A shadow blocks her lumen-sphere. 
18. She looks up to find Cyran standing in the aisle, absolutely silent, staring at the open ledger.
19. He snatches the book from the desk, slamming it shut with a crack that echoes like a gunshot.
20. He crowds her against the reading desk, his posture aggressive, defensive, and vibrating with humiliation.
21. He hurls a bitter accusation at her, his voice dripping with venom about her "hunger for scandal."
22. Lysa plants her hands on the desk, refusing to shrink away from his proximity or his anger.
23. She taps the cover of the closed ledger, stating firmly that she needs context to save him from Thessen.
24. Cyran paces a tight circle, mocking the idea that those pages contain the "truth" of who he is.
25. Lysa delivers her ultimatum, packs up her notebook, pushes past him, and leaves him standing alone with his own file.

*Note: Action focuses on the transition from quiet investigation to explosive discovery and confrontation.*

#### **Emotional Architecture**

**Emotional Beats (5-8 Key Moments):**

1. **Apprehension:** Entering the forbidden archives.
2. **Unease:** The encounter with Dr. Marrow—feeling perceived by a predator in a white coat.
3. **Intellectual Focus:** Translating the old records.
4. **Horror & Heartbreak:** Realizing the sheer scale and depravity of what Cyran survived, reframing his entire personality.
5. **Guilt/Shock:** Being caught reading his deepest traumas like a case study.
6. **Defensiveness (His):** Cyran lashing out to protect his exposed nerves.
7. **Refusal to Pity:** Lysa recognizing that apologizing or crying will only make him feel smaller. She chooses strength instead.
8. **The Challenge:** Leaving the ball in his court. He must choose to trust her.

**Character Arc Moment:**  
Lysa proves she won't coddle Cyran or treat him like a broken thing. She demands he take agency over his own history instead of letting the Tribunal's ledgers define him.

**Relationship Dynamics:**  
**Forced Transparency:** The illusion that Cyran was a willing participant in his past is permanently destroyed. The power dynamic shifts—she knows his darkest secrets, but refuses to weaponize them.

**Subtext Layer:**  
Cyran's anger isn't really that she *read* it; it's that she saw the proof that he was a victim, completely powerless. He is terrified she will look at him with pity.

**Character Interaction Beats:**

**Environmental Engagement:**

- **Objects touched:** The thick dust on the ledger, the delicate porcelain of the teacup, the brittle pages of the record.
